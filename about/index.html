Serving Adversary City since 1958.

A public trauma center built for the whole city, not just the neighborhoods that can reach it easily.

A hospital built to grow with the city.

City Hospital opened in 1958 as Adversary City General Hospital, a 120-bed facility on the edge of what was then the city limits. As Adversary City grew outward, so did the hospital: new wings, a dedicated trauma center, and a rebuild that brought total capacity to 340 beds.

Today it operates as the city's only Level II Trauma Center, run as a public facility under Adversary City's Department of Public Health rather than a private health system. Every department is staffed for around-the-clock coverage, and no patient is turned away from the Emergency Department for inability to pay.

History

Milestones since 1958.

  1. 1958

    Adversary City General Hospital opens

    The hospital opens with 120 beds on what was then the eastern edge of Adversary City.

  2. 1974

    East Wing and maternity ward added

    The East Wing opens, home to the city's first dedicated maternity ward.

  3. 1991

    Designated a Level II Trauma Center

    The Adversary Regional Health Board designates City Hospital the city's Level II Trauma Center.

  4. 2011

    Rebuild brings capacity to 340 beds

    A multi-year rebuild adds the current Radiology & Imaging suite and brings total capacity to 340 beds.

  5. 2023

    Neonatal intensive care unit opens

    The hospital opens a dedicated neonatal intensive care unit within Maternity & Newborn.
