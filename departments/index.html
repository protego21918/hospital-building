Every department at City Hospital.

Eight departments cover emergency care, specialist treatment, and diagnostics under one roof. Each one below lists its key services, hours, and the physician leading it.

Emergency & Trauma

City Hospital's Level II Trauma Center treats acute injury, chest pain, stroke, and critical illness around the clock. Ambulances arriving from anywhere in Adversary City are routed here first.

  • Trauma resuscitation
  • Chest pain protocol
  • Stroke response team
  • Poison control consultation
Open 24 hours Chief: Dr. Monica Geller

Cardiology

Diagnosis and treatment of heart conditions, from routine screening to interventional procedures. The catheterization lab runs alongside a dedicated cardiac recovery ward.

  • Echocardiography
  • Cardiac catheterization
  • Heart failure management
  • Cardiac rehabilitation
Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Emergency cardiac care available 24 hours through the ER. Chief: Dr. Sheldon Cooper

Maternity & Newborn

Labor, delivery, and newborn care in a wing built around families, with a neonatal intensive care unit for babies who need extra support.

  • Labor & delivery suites
  • Neonatal intensive care
  • Lactation consulting
  • Prenatal classes
Open 24 hours for deliveries. Tours by appointment, Mon–Sat. Chief: Dr. Rachel Green

Pediatrics

Primary and specialist care for infants, children, and adolescents, with exam rooms and imaging designed to feel less clinical for younger patients.

  • Well-child visits
  • Pediatric imaging
  • Vaccination clinic
  • Adolescent medicine
Mon–Sat, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM Chief: Dr. Amy Fowler

Oncology

Cancer screening, treatment planning, and ongoing infusion services, coordinated by a team that stays with patients from diagnosis through recovery.

  • Chemotherapy infusion
  • Radiation oncology referral
  • Cancer screening
  • Survivorship planning
Mon–Fri, 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM Chief: Dr. Leonard Hofstadter

Orthopedics & Sports Medicine

Joint reconstruction, fracture care, and rehabilitation for sports injuries, backed by an on-site physical therapy gym.

  • Joint replacement
  • Fracture clinic
  • Sports injury rehabilitation
  • Physical therapy
Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM Chief: Dr. Howard Wolowitz

Radiology & Imaging

X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound imaging read by on-site radiologists, with same-day results for most emergency and inpatient studies.

  • MRI & CT scanning
  • Ultrasound
  • Mammography
  • Interventional radiology
Open 24 hours for emergency imaging. Scheduled studies, Mon–Sat. Chief: Dr. Raj Koothrappali

Laboratory & Pathology

Bloodwork, diagnostic testing, and pathology services for every department, processed in a lab built for rapid turnaround on critical results.

  • Bloodwork & diagnostics
  • Pathology review
  • Blood bank
  • Rapid critical-result testing
Open 24 hours Chief: Dr. Bernadette Rostenkowski
City Hospital

Adversary City's public general hospital and Level II trauma center, serving the city since 1958.
