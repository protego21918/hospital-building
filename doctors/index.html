Emergency & Trauma

Dr. Sheldon Cooper

Cardiology MD, PhD, Board Certified in Cardiology · 12 years at City Hospital

Cardiology

Dr. Amy Fowler

Pediatrics MD, Board Certified in Pediatrics · 9 years at City Hospital

Pediatrics

Maternity & Newborn MD, Board Certified in Obstetrics & Gynecology · 10 years at City Hospital

Maternity & Newborn

Dr. Leonard Hofstadter

Oncology MD, Board Certified in Medical Oncology · 11 years at City Hospital

Oncology
