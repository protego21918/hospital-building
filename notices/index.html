Current advisories and updates from City Hospital.

Posted by department as conditions change. Check back before visiting if you have specific concerns about access or scheduling.

  • Jul 22, 2026

    Flu vaccination clinic open in the East Wing lobby

    Walk-in vaccinations are available through October, no appointment required. Bring an insurance card if you have one, though it isn't required to be seen.

  • Jul 15, 2026

    Blood donation drive, first Saturday of every month

    Donations are collected at the Meridian Parkway entrance from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. O-negative and platelet donors are especially needed this quarter.

  • Jul 8, 2026

    Visitor screening in place at all entrances

    During peak respiratory season, all visitors are asked to complete a short symptom screening before entering any ward.

  • Jun 30, 2026

    Parking structure, Level 2 closed for maintenance

    Level 2 of the visitor parking structure is closed through August 15. Levels 1, 3, and 4 remain open, and valet parking is available at the main entrance.

  • Jun 20, 2026

    New sign-in page for the patient portal

    MyCityHospital has a redesigned sign-in page. Existing accounts, saved messages, and test results are unaffected.

  • Jun 5, 2026

    Radiology now offers Saturday morning appointments

    Non-emergency imaging, including MRI and mammography, can now be scheduled on Saturday mornings between 8:00 AM and noon.

  • May 18, 2026

    City Hospital re-accredited as a Level II Trauma Center

    The Adversary Regional Health Board renewed City Hospital's Level II Trauma Center designation for another three years following its scheduled review.
