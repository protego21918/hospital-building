Patient Information

Getting ready for your visit.

  • Insurance & Billing

    Bring your insurance card and a photo ID to registration. Billing staff are available on the ground floor, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

  • Patient Portal

    MyCityHospital lets patients view test results and message their care team online. Ask registration staff for a sign-up code on your first visit.

  • Parking & Directions

    Visitor parking is on Meridian Parkway. Follow signs from the main entrance to the parking structure. Valet parking is available at the main entrance for a flat fee.

  • Interpreter Services

    Interpreters are available on request at registration for any department, including sign language interpretation with advance notice.

  • Financial Assistance

    Patients who qualify for the hospital's charity care program can apply at registration or by calling the billing office at 555-0142.

  • Discharge Planning

    A discharge coordinator works with every inpatient to arrange follow-up care, medical equipment, and transportation home.

  • Wi-Fi & Amenities

    Free guest Wi-Fi is available throughout the hospital. The ground-floor cafe is open 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily.
